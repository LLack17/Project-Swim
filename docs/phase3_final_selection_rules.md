# Phase 3 final selection (quality sleeve): rules fixed before the refreshed numbers

Written 2026-11-07, before the 13 quality pitches are refreshed and scored. Changes go in the amendment log at the bottom, dated, with the reason. The long-term growth sleeve (2 names) is chosen separately from its own five pitches; its names count toward the sector limits below.

## 1. Pass / fail first

A pick is out before scoring if its refreshed pitch shows a broken thesis (the event its own sell triggers name has happened), or a rule exclusion stands (corporate-action standard, 10% of market value). Each removal is written down with its reason.

## 2. Score (0-100)

Four measures, each turned into a percentile rank among the picks still in (0-100, ties averaged):

| Measure | Weight | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Expected 12-18 month return | 35% | Probability-weighted value (25% bear / 50% base / 25% bull) vs the refresh price, plus the next 12 months' dividend yield. Higher is better. |
| Downside | 25% | Bear-case value vs the refresh price. Smaller loss is better. |
| Durability | 25% | The 20-year view score (section 3). Higher is better. |
| Diversification | 15% | Correlation of daily returns with the S&P 500 over 3 years (factsheets.txt). Lower is better. |

Score = 0.35 x return rank + 0.25 x downside rank + 0.25 x durability rank + 0.15 x diversification rank.

## 3. Durability score (0-10), written in each pitch's "20-year view"

Five parts, 0, 1 or 2 points each, with one sentence of evidence per part:
1. **Lasting edge:** return on capital above its cost for most of the last 10 years, and market share or pricing holding (2 = clear and proven; 1 = likely; 0 = weak or eroding).
2. **Room to reinvest:** the business can keep putting money to work at good returns for 10+ years (2 / 1 / 0).
3. **Balance sheet:** survives a long recession without outside money (2 = net cash or net debt under 1.5x EBITDA; 1 = up to 3x; 0 = above 3x or refinancing risk).
4. **Management and capital allocation:** a record of sensible buybacks, dividends and deals, with no value-destroying acquisitions (2 / 1 / 0).
5. **Disruption risk:** how likely technology, regulation or a new competitor erodes the business over 20 years (2 = low; 1 = moderate; 0 = high).

## 4. Building the sleeve

- Rank by score. Take names from the top, skipping any that would break the mandate: at most 2 stocks per sector across both sleeves (quality and long-term growth), no sector above 20% of the portfolio.
- The quality sleeve holds 4-5 names. Landon chooses 4 or 5 and may replace a name with a written judgment reason; each cut gets one written line in the decision log.
- Entry follows the staged-buying rule (2026-11-01): half at launch, the rest over 3-6 months.
