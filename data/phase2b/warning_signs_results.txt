Notes: values are today's revised figures, not what was known at the time. Crashes cluster in time (1987, 2000-02,
2007-09 hit many countries at once), which is why the luck check shuffles blocks of years. Annual data misses

year-end prices with average-price inflation (standard JST practice). The luck check keeps missing years in place,
so blocks run 1986-90, 1991-95, ... for every country.
